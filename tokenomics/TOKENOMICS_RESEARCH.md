# Tokenomics research

Status: initial framing only; no token is launched or approved. A token may not be economically necessary. MANDAVEX core authorization must work without MDVX.

Research still required before any token design: compare infrastructure protocols with durable fee-backed usage and those whose activity depended on emissions/speculation; measure likely verification costs, verifier labor, security budget and user willingness to pay. Analyze validator capture, sybil resistance, dilution, vesting, treasury disclosure, liquidity and applicable legal constraints.

Do not infer token demand from hackathon transactions or promise appreciation, passive income or allocations. Prefer service fees and transparent credits if they solve actual needs. Proposed MDVX name/ticker and 100M supply are unverified and provisional.
