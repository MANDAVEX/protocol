## Holder onboarding: shortest meaningful task

1. Confirm Agent ID ownership in the official wallet and switch to BNB Smart Chain (chain 56).
2. Confirm Developer Mode is on and unexpired with `baw wallet settings --json`.
3. Review the mandate fields and task/artifact hash in the EIP-712 preview, then personally approve the signature request.
4. Review the exact Guard address, calldata, chain, task and fee in the contract-call preview, then personally approve execution.
5. For acceptance, a reviewer other than the contributor reviews the artifact, signs the acceptance mandate, reviews the exact call and approves it.
6. Verify each transaction hash with `node scripts/verify-receipt.cjs --tx <hash> --task-id <bytes32> --artifact-hash <bytes32> --task-status submitted|accepted` after the deployment manifest is updated. Independently compare the receipt on BscScan or a trusted BNB RPC.

A complete task lifecycle uses two EIP-712 signatures from the task owner (submission and acceptance authorization) and two on-chain transactions (contributor submission and reviewer acceptance). Owner and executors/reviewer must hold Agent ID as required by the Guard checks; the contributor cannot review their own submission. In the simplest arrangement, the owner can be the reviewer if they are not the contributor. Expected duration is **not yet measured with a genuine holder**; budget 5–10 minutes only as an unverified planning estimate, excluding artifact preparation and any wallet setup.
